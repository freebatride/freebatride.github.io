Questions Tucson Asks

Who is Uber Batman?

A Tucson rideshare driver in a Falcon-wing Tesla, turning ordinary Uber and Lyft trips into something people remember.

No. Tips are never an obligation on a free Bat Ride, but they are always appreciated.
